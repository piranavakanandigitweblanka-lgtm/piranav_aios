## Problem Statement

Polish (/pl/), Dutch (/nl/) and Spanish (/es/) locale URLs generated by Shopify Markets are appearing in UK Google Search Console with UK impressions, clicks, and average positions. Original ticket metrics: 2,612 locale URLs, 25,068 impressions, 20 clicks, 0.08% CTR over 28 days.

---

## Standing Constraint

> **DO NOT remove, noindex, or 301-redirect Polish, Dutch, or Spanish locale URLs.**  
> All 3 markets are required for international customers visiting ledsone.co.uk.  
> Fix path: improve hreflang + canonicals — not remove the markets.

---
