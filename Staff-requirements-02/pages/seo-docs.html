How it all fits together
Three data sources, one dashboard

The dashboard combines three separate data sources because no single tool gives the complete picture. Each source answers a different question:

Source 1 — Google (your own data)
Google Search Console and Google Ads. This is real data — actual clicks, actual spend. Only ledsone has access to this. Updated daily.
Source 2 — SEMrush (market data)
Third-party SEO tool. Estimated data — traffic models, keyword rankings, competitor snapshots. Covers the whole market, not just ledsone. Updated weekly.
Source 3 — Live website scan
The dashboard checks ledsone.co.uk's own website directly every time you open it. Used only for the AI Readiness score — detects schema markup and which AI bots are allowed.

Section 2
Google Search Console
Your actual Google search performance — the only source of real click data
Daily refreshReal clicks — not estimates

Google Search Console (GSC) is ledsone's own data, direct from Google. Every time someone searches Google and clicks a ledsone result, that click is recorded here. This is the only number on the dashboard that represents real traffic. Every other traffic figure (SEMrush, competitor estimates) is a model — GSC is ground truth.

The data arrives daily. However, Google adds a 2–3 day delay before making it available, so today's clicks won't appear until Wednesday or Thursday.

Where you see GSC data in the dashboard
Dashboard sectionWhat the number means
Overview → Organic ClicksTotal times someone clicked a ledsone result in Google this month
Overview → Avg PositionAverage ranking position across all keywords ledsone appears for
Keyword Intelligence → Top KeywordsWhich search queries drive the most clicks, plus position, impressions, and CTR
Keyword Intelligence → Rising / DecliningQueries where your ranking improved or dropped compared to last month
Keyword Intelligence → Top PagesWhich pages on ledsone.co.uk receive the most Google clicks
Month-on-Month table → GSC Clicks columnMonthly click totals for the past 6 months
⚠️ Why the keyword table shows fewer clicks than the Overview total: Google groups very low-volume queries into a "(not provided)" bucket and doesn't show them individually. The Overview KPI counts everything; the keyword table only shows the queries Google chooses to report. This is a Google limitation — not a dashboard bug.
💡 Why use GSC AND SEMrush? GSC shows what Google actually sent you — but only for keywords you already rank for. SEMrush estimates total organic potential and shows keywords you should be targeting. Together they give a complete picture of current performance and future opportunity.
Technical details — GSC pipeline

Data is stored in the Ledsone PostgreSQL server (same server as Google Ads). Tables used: gsc_daily_stats (site-level clicks/impressions/CTR/position aggregated by date), gsc_query_stats (per-query breakdown with page URL). The background pipeline ingests the previous day via GSC API each morning. The api/seo.js module ?module=overview and ?module=keywords query these tables directly. No caching — all queries run on request.

Section 3
SEMrush Organic Data
Estimated traffic trend, keyword rankings, and 37-month history
Weekly — every MondayEstimates — not real traffic

SEMrush estimates how much organic traffic a website receives by looking at every keyword it ranks for and applying average click-through rates for each position. These are estimates, not real traffic numbers. The actual traffic could be 20–30% higher or lower. But because SEMrush uses the same model for every website, the estimates are consistent — which makes them useful for tracking trends and comparing against competitors.

A cloud agent runs every Monday morning and fetches the latest SEMrush data, storing it for the dashboard to read. This is why SEMrush data on the dashboard is typically 1–7 days old.

Section 4
Google Ads
Monthly ad spend, return on ad spend (ROAS), and conversion value — UK account
Daily refreshReal spend data

The Ads data comes from ledsone's own Google Ads account — it is real spend and conversion data, not an estimate. It covers the UK account from February 2020 to present and is updated daily by the same pipeline that brings in the GSC data.

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) is the key efficiency metric. A ROAS of 4 means for every £1 spent on ads, £4 of revenue was generated. It is calculated as: Conversion Value ÷ Ad Cost.

The Ads overlay chart on the dashboard lets you see whether paid investment is paying off and whether ad spend periods correlate with organic traffic movements — high spend on brand terms can lift organic click-through rates too.

Where you see Ads data

Technical details — Ads pipeline

Stored in Ledsone PostgreSQL alongside GSC. Table: google_ads_monthly — columns: month, cost_gbp, conversion_value, conversions, roas. The daily pipeline aggregates the previous day and upserts into the monthly rollup. API module: ?module=ads in api/seo.js.

Backlinks & Domain Authority
Who links to ledsone.co.uk and how the domain's authority is trending
Weekly — every Monday

A backlink is when another website links to ledsone.co.uk. Google treats backlinks as votes of confidence — a link from a reputable website tells Google that ledsone is a trustworthy, relevant source. The more high-quality websites that link to ledsone, the stronger the domain becomes and the easier it is to rank for competitive keywords.

What matters most is referring domains — the number of unique websites linking to ledsone. One website linking 50 times counts far less than 50 different websites each linking once.

⚠️ Critical issue detected: An 86% drop in backlinks has been identified between the 2024 peak and the most recent snapshot. This is flagged on the dashboard because a sustained link loss of this size typically causes ranking drops within 2–4 months if not addressed.

Competitor Traffic Comparison
ledsone.co.uk versus the top 5 UK lighting competitors — monthly estimated organic traffic
Weekly — every MondayEstimates

This chart answers the most important strategic question: are we growing relative to the market, or are competitors pulling ahead?

A rising ledsone line against flat competitor lines means you are gaining market share. A flat ledsone line against rising competitor lines means you are losing ground — even if your absolute traffic looks stable, competitors are capturing new demand that you are not. You need this context to know whether a traffic change is a ledsone issue or a market-wide shift.

The five competitors tracked are: lightingcompany.co.uk, ledhut.co.uk, industville.co.uk, vintagelightbulb.co.uk, lampspares.co.uk. All figures are SEMrush estimates — consistent enough for meaningful comparison even if not perfectly accurate.

Keyword Gap vs ledhut.co.uk
Keywords where ledhut ranks in the top 10 but ledsone does not — your most actionable content opportunities
Weekly — every Monday

The keyword gap is a list of search terms that ledhut.co.uk ranks in Google's top 10 for, but ledsone.co.uk does not rank at all (or ranks below position 20). These are proven, real keywords that UK shoppers are searching for in the lighting market — ledhut is already getting traffic from them. The question is: why isn't ledsone?

Each keyword on the list represents a content opportunity. If ledsone creates or improves a page targeting that keyword, it could capture the same traffic ledhut is currently getting.

Section 8
AI Readiness Score (GEO)
How ready ledsone.co.uk is to be cited by ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity
Real-time on every loadManual checklist items

GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) is about being recommended by AI, not just ranked by Google. When someone asks ChatGPT "what's the best place to buy LED lights in the UK?", ChatGPT doesn't search Google — it searches its own knowledge and the web for authoritative sources to cite. The AI Readiness score measures how likely ledsone.co.uk is to be one of those citations.

Unlike traditional SEO (which updates weekly), the AI Readiness score recalculates every time you open the dashboard by scanning ledsone.co.uk directly. This means changes you make to the website today appear in the score immediately.

Technical details — GEO scoring

Automatic Data Refresh
4 cloud agents that keep SEMrush data up to date every Monday morning
Every Monday 03:00 UTC

SEMrush doesn't push data to us automatically, so four cloud agents run every Monday at 03:00 UTC (08:30 Sri Lanka time). Each agent connects to SEMrush, fetches the latest data for its specific task, and stores a new snapshot. The dashboard then reads those snapshots.

If an agent run fails one week, the dashboard shows the previous week's data — it does not go blank. Each dataset is timestamped so you can always see how old it is.

3 Backlinks & Authority Total backlinks, referring domains, authority score Backlinks Health charts, Backlinks KPIs

💡 To check agent status or run manually: Go to claude.ai/code/routines. You can see when each agent last ran, whether it succeeded, and trigger a manual run if you need a mid-week refresh.
Technical details — agent configuration

All 4 agents are Claude Code cloud routines (CCRs) running on the claude-sonnet-4-6 model. Each agent checks out the piranav_aios GitHub repo and runs a self-contained prompt that uses the SEMrush MCP connector (connector_uuid: 56a453d4-a573-4213-8e58-130c6a68c10c) and writes directly to Neon via the connection string. Cron expression for all 4: 0 3 * * 1 (Monday 03:00 UTC). Agent IDs are visible at claude.ai/code/routines.

Glossary
Plain-English definitions of every term used in the dashboard
TermWhat it means in plain English
Authority Score (AS)SEMrush's measure (0–100) of how trusted a domain is on the internet. Built from the quality and quantity of websites linking to it. Higher score = easier to rank in Google.
BacklinkA link from another website pointing to ledsone.co.uk. Treated by Google as a vote of confidence.
ClicksHow many times someone clicked a ledsone result in Google. From GSC — this is real data.
CTR (Click-Through Rate)Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100. A 5% CTR means 5 out of every 100 people who saw the ledsone listing clicked it.
GEOGenerative Engine Optimisation. Making your website ready to be cited and recommended by AI systems like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews — not just ranked by Google Search.
ImpressionsHow many times a ledsone result appeared on a Google results page. Someone seeing it but not clicking still counts as an impression. From GSC — real data.
IntentThe reason behind a search. Transactional = ready to buy ("buy LED bulbs"). Commercial = comparing options ("best LED strip lights UK"). Informational = learning ("how do LED lights work"). Navigational = looking for a specific brand.
KD (Keyword Difficulty)SEMrush's score (0–100) for how hard it is to rank on page 1 of Google for a keyword. Under 30 = achievable in months. Over 70 = requires significant authority and long-term effort.
Keyword GapKeywords where a competitor ranks in Google's top 10 but ledsone does not rank in the top 20. Each gap keyword is a content opportunity.
Organic TrafficVisitors who arrive at ledsone.co.uk by clicking a free (non-ad) Google search result. As opposed to paid traffic (ads) or direct traffic (typing the URL).
Opportunity ScoreA number that ranks keyword gap entries by potential impact. Calculated as: search volume × (10 ÷ competitor's ranking position). Higher = better opportunity.
PositionWhere a page ranks in Google results for a specific search term. Position 1 = top result. Position 10 = bottom of page 1. Position 11+ = page 2 (almost no clicks).
Referring DomainsThe number of unique websites that link to ledsone.co.uk. More important than total backlink count — 50 different sites linking once is stronger than 1 site linking 50 times.
ROASReturn on Ad Spend. Conversion Value ÷ Ad Cost. ROAS of 4 = £4 revenue generated for every £1 spent on Google Ads.
Schema / Structured DataSpecial invisible code added to web pages that tells Google and AI systems exactly what a page contains — whether it's a product, an FAQ, a review, or an organisation. Makes pages easier for machines to understand.
SEMrush Traffic EstimateSEMrush's calculation of how much organic traffic a domain receives per month, based on its keyword rankings and typical click rates for each position. An estimate — not real traffic. Useful for trends and comparisons.
sameAsA way of telling AI systems and Google "this is also us" by linking your website's structured data to your Wikipedia page, Wikidata entry, or LinkedIn profile. Helps AI systems confidently identify who ledsone is.
WikidataA free, publicly editable knowledge base used by Wikipedia, Google, and AI systems like ChatGPT to store facts about people, organisations, and places. Having a Wikidata entry makes it easier for AI to reference ledsone.
