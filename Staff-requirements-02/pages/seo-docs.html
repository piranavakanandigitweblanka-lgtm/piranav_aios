Section 1
Overview & Architecture
How all three data layers connect to build the dashboard

The SEO dashboard pulls from three independent data layers. Each layer has a different update cadence and serves a different purpose. No single source gives the full picture — combining them is what makes the dashboard useful.

Layer 1 — Live PostgreSQL
Ledsone's own server. Stores Google Search Console clicks, impressions, positions and Google Ads spend/ROAS. Updated daily by a background pipeline.
Layer 2 — Neon PostgreSQL
Cloud database (eu-west-2). Stores SEMrush snapshots: organic traffic history, keyword rankings, backlinks, competitor traffic, keyword gap. Refreshed weekly by cloud agents.
Layer 3 — Live Fetches
On every page load, the API fetches ledsone.co.uk's robots.txt and homepage HTML directly to score AI Readiness in real time.

Section 2
Google Search Console
Your actual Google search performance — clicks, impressions, positions, pages
Source
Google Search Console API → Ledsone PostgreSQL pipeline
Refresh
Daily Background pipeline ingests previous day's data each morning
Delay
GSC data is 2–3 days behind. Today's clicks won't appear until Wednesday/Thursday.
Coverage
All queries and pages that appeared in Google Search for ledsone.co.uk

GSC is the only source of real click data. Every other tool (SEMrush, Ahrefs) estimates traffic. GSC shows what Google actually sent you. The dashboard uses it for keyword rankings (position per query), top landing pages, and month-on-month click/impression trends.

⚠️ GSC sampling cap: queries with very low volume are grouped under "(other)" and not shown individually. The dashboard shows ~50% of true total clicks in the keyword table because long-tail queries are aggregated by Google.

💡 Why GSC and SEMrush both? GSC gives real clicks but only for queries you already rank for. SEMrush estimates all organic traffic and shows keywords you should be targeting. Together they give a complete view.

Backlinks & Domain Authority
Who links to ledsone.co.uk, authority trend, and the 86% backlink drop alert
Source

Refresh
Weekly Cloud Agent 3 runs every Monday 03:00 UTC
Table
semrush_backlinks in Neon — monthly snapshots of total_backlinks, referring_domains, authority_score
Coverage
Aug 2024 – present (24-month window)

Backlinks are one of Google's strongest ranking signals. The number of unique referring domains matters more than total backlink count (one domain linking 100 times counts less than 100 different domains each linking once). The dashboard tracks both.

An 86% backlink drop has been detected between peak (2024) and the most recent snapshot. This is flagged as a critical alert because sudden link loss can cause significant ranking drops 2–4 months later.

Keyword Gap vs ledhut.co.uk
Keywords where ledhut ranks top-10 but ledsone doesn't — your biggest content opportunities
Source
SEMrush keyword gap analysis (MCP organic_research) → Neon PostgreSQL
Refresh
Weekly Cloud Agent 4 runs every Monday 03:00 UTC
Table
semrush_keyword_gap in Neon — keyword, competitor_position, volume, KD, intent, ledsone_position, opportunity_score
Opportunity Score
volume × (10 ÷ competitor_position). Higher = bigger traffic gain if you rank where they do.

The keyword gap shows you exactly what to write next. Each row is a keyword that ledhut.co.uk already ranks in the top 10 for, but ledsone.co.uk does not rank at all (or ranks outside position 20). If ledhut can rank there, so can ledsone — these keywords are proven to convert in the UK lighting market.

How Opportunity Score is calculated
ComponentMeaning
volumeMonthly search volume for the keyword (UK)
competitor_positionWhere ledhut.co.uk ranks (1–10). Position 1 = 10 points weight, position 10 = 1 point.
Opportunity Scorevolume × (10 ÷ position). A keyword with 5,000 volume where ledhut ranks #2 = 5,000 × 5 = 25,000
💡 Sort by Opportunity Score to find the highest-impact keywords first. Filter to KD < 30 for "easy win" opportunities where a new or updated page could rank within 3–6 months.

Section 8
AI Readiness Score (GEO)
How ready ledsone.co.uk is to be cited by ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity
Source — Live
Fetches ledsone.co.uk/robots.txt and homepage HTML on every page load
Source — DB
semrush_history (authority), semrush_backlinks (AS), geo_checklist (manual items) from Neon
Refresh
Real-time Scores recalculate on every dashboard load. No cache.
Manual items
Manual 8 checklist items you toggle in the dashboard when completed

GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) is the new frontier beyond traditional SEO. AI systems like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews choose which websites to cite based on signals that are different from classic ranking factors — they look for structured data, entity clarity, E-E-A-T signals, and whether their crawlers are allowed.

The 7 scored dimensions (each 0–10)
DimensionWhat it measuresHow it's calculated
Crawler AccessAre AI bots allowed to crawl the site?Live robots.txt fetch — checks GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended. +2 per allowed bot.
AuthorityDoes the domain have enough trust for AI to cite it?SEMrush Authority Score from Neon. AS 70+ = 10/10, scaled down below.
Entity ClarityDoes AI know clearly what this business is?Live homepage parse — checks for sameAs links (Wikipedia, Wikidata, Companies House), Organization schema.
Content FormatIs content structured in formats AI can extract?Presence of FAQPage, HowTo, Article schema in ld+json. Plus blog post count (Neon checklist).
E-E-A-TDoes the site demonstrate expertise and trust signals?AggregateRating schema, author schema, credentials page (checklist items).
Organic FootprintDoes the site appear across enough search queries?SEMrush organic keyword count from Neon — more keywords = wider AI exposure surface.
Structured DataIs there enough machine-readable markup?Count of distinct schema types found in ld+json on homepage.
The 8 manual checklist items
ItemWhat to doWhy it matters
FAQPage SchemaAdd FAQ markup to key product/category pagesAI systems extract FAQ content directly to answer user questions
Aggregate RatingAdd review schema to product pagesTrust signal — AI prefers citing sites with social proof
sameAs — OrgAdd sameAs links to Wikipedia/Wikidata in Organization schemaConnects ledsone to a known entity in the knowledge graph
Wikidata EntityCreate a Wikidata entry for ledsone.co.ukWikidata is the primary knowledge graph used by AI systems
Author SchemaAdd author markup to blog postsE-E-A-T: real authors = real expertise
Credentials PageCreate a dedicated /about or /expertise pageE-E-A-T: explicit expertise signals are weighted heavily
Blog 30+ PostsPublish at least 30 blog articlesContent breadth = wider AI citation surface
Link Building 5+Acquire 5+ new referring domains this quarterCounteracts the 86% backlink drop and improves authority
💡 The total GEO score (0–100) is the sum of all 7 dimension scores × 100/70. A score above 60 means you are reasonably well positioned to be cited by AI. Below 40 means AI systems are likely ignoring ledsone.co.uk even when it's the most relevant answer.

Section 9
Cloud Refresh Agents
4 automated agents that keep the SEMrush data in Neon up to date every Monday

Because SEMrush does not offer a real-time push API, four cloud Claude agents run every Monday at 03:00 UTC (08:30 IST). Each agent connects to SEMrush via MCP, fetches the latest data, and writes it to the Neon PostgreSQL database. The dashboard then reads from Neon.

#Agent NameWhat it fetchesWhere it writesSchedule
1 Keywords & History Refresh domain_overview (traffic, AS, keyword count) + resource_organic top 50 keywords semrush_history, semrush_keywords (Neon) Mon 03:00 UTC
2 Competitor Traffic Snapshots domain_overview for 5 competitors (lightingcompany, ledhut, industville, vintagelightbulb, lampspares) semrush_competitors (Neon) Mon 03:00 UTC
3 Backlinks & Authority Snapshot backlinks_research — total backlinks, referring domains, authority score semrush_backlinks (Neon) Mon 03:00 UTC
4 Keyword Gap Refresh organic_research for ledhut.co.uk top 100 keywords, compares ledsone positions semrush_keyword_gap (Neon) Mon 03:00 UTC
⚠️ If a Monday agent fails, the dashboard shows the previous week's data — it does not go blank. Each table stores the latest snapshot_date so you can always see when data was last refreshed.

Agents can be viewed and managed at claude.ai/code/routines. You can also trigger a manual run from there if you need a mid-week refresh.

Section 10
Glossary
Plain-English definitions of every term used in the dashboard
TermPlain English definition
Authority Score (AS)SEMrush's 0–100 measure of how trusted a domain is. Higher = more ranking power. Built from backlink quality, traffic, and spam signals.
ClicksHow many times someone clicked a ledsone.co.uk result in Google. Source: Google Search Console (real data).
CTRClick-Through Rate. Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100. A 5% CTR means 5 in every 100 people who saw the result clicked it.
GEOGenerative Engine Optimisation. The practice of optimising a website to be cited by AI systems (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity), not just ranked in Google.
ImpressionsHow many times a ledsone.co.uk result appeared in Google search results. Source: GSC (real data).
IntentWhy someone searched. Commercial = comparing products. Transactional = ready to buy. Informational = researching. Navigational = looking for a specific site.
KD (Keyword Difficulty)SEMrush's 0–100 score for how hard it is to rank for a keyword. Under 30 = relatively easy. Over 70 = very competitive.
Keyword GapKeywords where a competitor ranks in the top 10 but you do not rank at all or rank outside position 20.
ld+jsonA way of embedding structured data (Schema.org) in a web page's HTML so search engines and AI systems can understand what the page is about.
MCPModel Context Protocol. A standard that lets Claude agents connect to external tools (like SEMrush) and use them directly.
Neon DBA cloud PostgreSQL database (hosted in eu-west-2 AWS). Stores all SEMrush snapshots and the GEO checklist.
Opportunity Scorevolume × (10 ÷ competitor_position). Ranks keyword gap entries by potential traffic impact. Higher is better.
PositionWhere a URL ranks in Google search results for a query. Position 1 = top result. Position 10 = bottom of page 1.
ROASReturn on Ad Spend. Conversion Value ÷ Ad Cost. ROAS of 4 means you made £4 revenue for every £1 spent on ads.
Referring DomainsNumber of unique websites that have at least one link pointing to ledsone.co.uk. More unique domains = stronger authority signal.
sameAsA Schema.org property that links your Organization to its profiles on other authoritative sites (Wikipedia, Wikidata, LinkedIn). Helps AI systems identify who you are.
SEMrush Traffic Est.SEMrush's estimate of how much organic traffic a domain receives per month. Based on keyword rankings × average CTR for each position. Not real traffic — a model.
VercelThe hosting platform where the SEO dashboard (seo.html) and the API (api/seo.js) are deployed. Free Hobby plan — deployments must be triggered manually with vercel --prod.
