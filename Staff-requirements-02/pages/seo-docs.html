37-Month Trend Chart

Shows ledsone's estimated organic traffic and keyword count from July 2023 to present. Use this to spot long-term growth or decline — a single bad month is noise, a 3-month downward trend is a signal. Source: SEMrush weekly snapshots stored in the cloud database.

Google Ads Overlay Chart

Monthly ad spend (bars) and ROAS (line) from February 2020 to present. Lets you see whether paid investment is paying off and whether spend spikes correlate with organic traffic changes. Source: Google Ads pipeline, updated daily.

Competitor Traffic Comparison Chart

ledsone vs 5 competitors (lightingcompany, ledhut, industville, vintagelightbulb, lampspares) — estimated monthly organic traffic. The key question is not "is our traffic going up?" but "are we growing faster or slower than competitors?" Source: SEMrush weekly snapshots, refreshed every Monday.

💡 Date range filter: The date picker at the top of the dashboard controls the GSC date range for KPIs and keyword tables. It does not affect SEMrush or Ads data — those always show the full available history.
Technical details — Phase 1 API calls

API modules: ?module=overview (KPIs + trend), ?module=ads (Ads chart), ?module=competitors (competitor chart), ?module=backlinks (backlinks charts). Overview queries both Ledsone PostgreSQL (GSC tables) and Neon (semrush_history, semrush_backlinks). Competitor data from Neon semrush_competitors. All chart data is returned as JSON arrays keyed by month (YYYY-MM-DD).

Phase 2
Product Intelligence
Which individual products and collections are driving organic traffic and clicks
GSC — Daily

This tab breaks down SEO performance at the product level rather than site-wide. You can see which specific product pages or collection pages are pulling in the most Google traffic — and which ones with good rankings are failing to convert that into clicks.

What you can do in this tab
What you seeWhat it tells youAction to take
Products with high impressions, low clicksGoogle is showing the page but people aren't clicking — the title or meta description is weakRewrite the meta title and description to be more compelling
Products with good position (1–10) but 0 clicksRanking well but something is stopping clicks — possibly a rich snippet competitor is stealing attentionAdd review schema, FAQ schema, or improve the snippet
Products with declining positionRanking is slipping — possible competitor improvement or content freshness issueUpdate product description, add more content, check for cannibalization
Products with 0 impressionsGoogle isn't indexing or ranking this page at allCheck indexation in Technical SEO tab, check internal linking
💡 All data is from Google Search Console — real clicks and impressions, not estimates. Filtered to product and collection URL patterns (/products/ and /collections/).
Technical details — Phase 2 API

API module: ?module=products. Queries Ledsone PostgreSQL google_search_console.page table, filtered with ILIKE '%/products/%' OR ILIKE '%/collections/%'. Aggregates clicks, impressions, CTR, avg_position by page URL over the global date range. Shopify product handle extracted from URL path for display.

Phase 3
Keyword Intelligence
6 sub-tabs covering keyword rankings, opportunities, trends, and competitor gaps
GSC — Daily SEMrush — Weekly

The most detailed tab in the dashboard. Split into 6 sub-tabs covering different angles of keyword performance — from what's ranking today to what you should be targeting next.

Top Keywords Opportunities ↑ Rising ↓ Declining SEMrush Rankings 🔓 Keyword Gap
Top Keywords — GSC

All queries ledsone currently ranks for, sorted by clicks. Source: Google Search Console — real click data. Each row shows the search term, clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position. You can sort by any column and search by keyword. The intent filter (Transactional / Commercial / Informational) classifies why someone searched that term.

Also shows which landing page received the click for each keyword — useful for checking if the right page is ranking for the right term.

Opportunities — GSC

Keywords where ledsone ranks in positions 4–20 — meaning Google thinks the page is relevant but it's not getting much traffic yet. These are the highest-value quick wins: a position improvement from 8 to 3 can multiply clicks by 4x without creating any new content. Just improving the existing page (freshness, internal links, better content) is often enough.

↑ Rising — GSC

Keywords where ledsone's ranking position improved this month compared to last month. Positive signals — shows which pages Google is rewarding. Use this to understand what's working so you can replicate it on weaker pages.

↓ Declining — GSC

Keywords where ranking position dropped this month. These need attention — a ranking drop today leads to a traffic drop in 2–4 weeks. Check if a competitor updated their page, if the ledsone page lost backlinks, or if the content has gone stale.

SEMrush Rankings — SEMrush

The top 50 keywords SEMrush currently shows ledsone ranking for in the UK, with estimated search volume, Keyword Difficulty (KD), and search intent. Source: SEMrush — refreshed every Monday. Use this to see keywords that GSC might not show (low-volume queries that fall under Google's privacy threshold).

🔓 Keyword Gap — SEMrush

Keywords where ledhut.co.uk ranks in the top 10 but ledsone does not rank at all (or ranks below position 20). These are proven UK lighting keywords with real search volume — ledhut is already getting traffic from them. Each row shows the Opportunity Score (volume × 10/competitor position) so you can prioritise which gaps to close first.

Source: SEMrush, refreshed every Monday by Cloud Agent 4. Sort by Opportunity Score and filter KD < 30 to find the easiest wins.

Technical details — Phase 3 API

GSC sub-tabs: ?module=keywords&type=top|opportunity|rising|declining — queries Ledsone PostgreSQL google_search_console.query table. Rising/declining compare current vs previous month using window functions. SEMrush sub-tab: ?module=semrush&type=semrush — reads semrush_keywords from Neon (latest snapshot_date). Keyword Gap: ?module=semrush&type=keyword-gap — reads semrush_keyword_gap from Neon. Opportunity score: volume × (10 / competitor_position).

Phase 4
Landing Pages
4 sub-tabs showing page-level performance, type breakdown, month-on-month changes, and zero-click audits
GSC — Daily SEMrush Pages — Weekly

Where the Keyword Intelligence tab shows performance by search term, this tab shows performance by URL. Every page on ledsone.co.uk that has appeared in Google results is listed here. Click any page URL to open it in a new tab. Click ▶ Trend to expand a month-by-month history for that page inline.

GSC All Pages By Page Type Month-on-Month SEMrush Pages
GSC All Pages

Every URL on ledsone.co.uk that Google has shown in search results, sorted by clicks. Columns: page URL (clickable), page type (Product / Collection / Other), clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, days with data, and a ▶ Trend button.

▶ Trend button: Click it to expand a monthly breakdown for that page showing clicks, impressions, and position month by month. Click again to collapse. This lets you see if a page's traffic is growing, steady, or declining over time without leaving the tab.

By Page Type

Groups pages into Products, Collections, and Other pages and shows aggregate performance for each group. Useful for understanding whether product pages or category pages are driving more traffic, and which group is improving or declining.

Month-on-Month

Shows each page's performance this month versus last month — clicks change, position change, impressions change. Sorted by biggest movers. Use this to quickly find pages that suddenly dropped or spiked, which is often a sign of a Google algorithm update affecting specific page types.

SEMrush Pages — Zero-Click Audit

Pages that have high impressions in Google but zero or very low clicks. These pages are visible on Google but failing to attract any traffic. The audit shows why and suggests a fix — for example, if a page ranks position 5–10 with zero clicks, the meta title/description needs rewriting to be more compelling.

Source: SEMrush page-level data, refreshed weekly.

💡 Page URLs are clickable links — click any URL in the table to open that ledsone page in a new tab. Use this to quickly review the page content alongside its SEO data.
Technical details — Phase 4 API

GSC sub-tabs: ?module=landing&type=pages|by-type|top-pages — queries Ledsone PostgreSQL google_search_console.page table. Trend endpoint: ?module=landing&type=page-trend&page=URL — returns monthly aggregates for a single page URL. SEMrush Pages: ?module=landing&type=semrush-pages — reads from Neon semrush_keywords table, grouped by URL with zero-click filter. Page type classification via URL pattern matching (/products/, /collections/).

Phase 5
Action Centre
Prioritised list of the highest-impact SEO tasks right now — what to work on this week
GSC — Daily SEMrush — Weekly

The Action Centre takes data from every other tab and turns it into a prioritised task list. Instead of spending time analysing charts, you open this tab and see exactly what to do — ranked by impact.

Each action item shows: what the issue is, which page it affects, why it matters (the impact), and what to do. Items are colour-coded by priority — red is critical, amber is high priority, green is improvement opportunity.

Technical details — Phase 5 API

API module: ?module=actions. Runs multiple sub-queries across both databases and synthesises into a ranked action list. Priority scoring: critical (score > 80), high (50–80), medium (20–50). Actions are generated server-side from live data on each request — not stored.

Phase 6
Technical SEO
3 sub-tabs: index coverage, keyword cannibalization, and zero-click page audit
GSC — Daily

Technical SEO issues are often invisible — pages that aren't indexed, keywords where two ledsone pages compete against each other, or pages that rank but never get clicked. This tab surfaces those hidden problems.

Index Coverage Cannibalization Zero-Click Pages
Index Coverage

Shows how many ledsone pages Google has indexed broken down by type (products, collections, other pages). If the number of indexed products is much lower than the total products in your Shopify store, some products are not appearing in Google at all — they need to be fixed before any other optimisation will help them.

Also shows the date range of GSC data available, so you know how current the coverage picture is.

Cannibalization

Keyword cannibalization happens when two or more ledsone pages both try to rank for the same search term and end up competing against each other. This confuses Google and usually means both pages rank lower than one well-optimised page would on its own. This tab identifies keyword-page pairs where this is happening so you can merge or redirect the weaker page.

Zero-Click Pages

Pages that appear in Google results (impressions > 0) but receive zero clicks. High impressions + zero clicks usually means the meta title or description is not matching what the user was looking for, or a competitor's snippet is far more attractive. Each page is shown with a suggested fix.

Technical details — Phase 6 API

API module: ?module=technical&type=coverage|cannibalization|zero-click. All queries run against Ledsone PostgreSQL google_search_console schema. Cannibalization query: finds keywords where the same query maps to 2+ distinct page URLs with clicks in the selected date range. Zero-click: SUM(impressions) > 50 AND SUM(clicks) = 0 grouped by page, ordered by impressions desc.

Phase 7
AI Readiness (GEO)
Live score showing how ready ledsone.co.uk is to be cited by ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity
Live — recalculates on every load Manual checklist

GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) is about being recommended by AI, not just ranked by Google. When someone asks ChatGPT "best place to buy LED lights in the UK?", ChatGPT looks for authoritative, well-structured websites to cite. This tab measures how ready ledsone.co.uk is for that.

Unlike every other tab, the score recalculates every time you open this tab by scanning the live ledsone.co.uk website. Changes you make today appear in the score immediately.

Technical details — Phase 7 API

API module: ?module=geo. Live fetches: https://ledsone.co.uk/robots.txt (AbortSignal 6s) + homepage HTML (8s). Parses ld+json blocks, sameAs arrays, OG tags, robots rules for GPTBot/ChatGPT-User/ClaudeBot/PerplexityBot/Google-Extended. DB reads from Neon: semrush_history (latest AS + keyword count), semrush_backlinks (latest AS), geo_checklist (8 manual items). Toggle endpoint: POST ?module=geo&type=toggle&key=X&done=Y — updates geo_checklist.done in Neon. Score = sum of 7 dimensions × (100/70).

Glossary
Plain-English definitions of every term used in the dashboard
Authority Score (AS)
SEMrush's 0–100 measure of domain trust. Built from backlink quality and traffic signals. Higher = easier to rank in Google.
Backlink
A link from another website pointing to ledsone.co.uk. Treated by Google as a vote of confidence.
Cannibalization
When two ledsone pages compete for the same keyword, splitting authority and both ranking lower than one strong page would.
Clicks
How many times someone clicked a ledsone result in Google search. From GSC — real data, not an estimate.
CTR (Click-Through Rate)
Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100. A 5% CTR means 5 of every 100 people who saw the result clicked it.
E-E-A-T
Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness — Google and AI systems use these signals to judge whether a site is a credible source.
GEO
Generative Engine Optimisation. Making your website ready to be cited by AI systems like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews.
Impressions
How many times a ledsone result appeared on a Google results page. Seeing it without clicking still counts. From GSC — real data.
Intent
Why someone searched. Transactional = ready to buy. Commercial = comparing options. Informational = learning. Navigational = finding a specific site.
KD (Keyword Difficulty)
SEMrush's 0–100 score for how hard it is to rank on page 1 of Google. Under 30 = achievable. Over 70 = very competitive.
Keyword Gap
Keywords where a competitor ranks top-10 but ledsone doesn't appear in the top 20. Each is a proven content opportunity.
Opportunity Score
Volume × (10 ÷ competitor position). Ranks keyword gap entries by potential traffic impact. Higher = bigger opportunity.
Position
Where a page ranks in Google results. Position 1 = top result. Position 10 = bottom of page 1. Position 11+ = page 2 (almost no clicks).
Referring Domains
Number of unique websites linking to ledsone. 50 different sites linking once is much stronger than 1 site linking 50 times.
ROAS
Return on Ad Spend = Conversion Value ÷ Ad Cost. ROAS of 4 means £4 revenue for every £1 spent on Google Ads.
sameAs
Schema.org property linking your website's Organization data to your Wikipedia, Wikidata, or LinkedIn page. Helps AI identify who ledsone is.
Schema / Structured Data
Invisible code added to web pages that tells Google and AI exactly what a page contains — product, FAQ, review, organisation. Makes pages machine-readable.
SEMrush Traffic Estimate
SEMrush's model of how much organic traffic a domain receives. Based on rankings × average CTR per position. Not real traffic — an estimate. Good for trends and comparisons.
Wikidata
A free public knowledge base used by Wikipedia, Google, and AI systems to store facts about organisations. Having an entry helps AI confidently reference ledsone.
Zero-Click Page
A page that appears in Google results (impressions) but receives no clicks. High impressions + zero clicks = the snippet isn't compelling enough.
