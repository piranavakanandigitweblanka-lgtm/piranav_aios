IP Infringement — Analysis & Action Plan

1. Site Identification

Ours — LEDSone
  • ledsone.co.uk — UK store
  • ledsone.de — German market
  • Address: Schmutter Str. 16, 78647 Trossingen, Germany
  • Contact: admin@ledsone.de / jefri@ledsone.de
  • Full legal transparency — VAT, address, registration
  • Same product range: pendant lights, LED bulbs, vintage lighting
Infringing — Lidsone
  • lidsone.com
  • Language: German  |  Currency: EUR
  • Contact: support@lidsone.com
  • No address listed
  • No VAT number listed
  • No company registration listed
  • Using our logo without authorisation

2. Legal Claims

Claim Strength Basis
Copyright infringement (logo) Strong Our logo is original creative work — automatically protected under UK & EU copyright law. Lidsone reproduced it without licence or authorisation.
Passing off / Trade dress Strong Similar name (lidsone vs ledsone) + copied logo = deliberately misleading consumers into believing an association with LEDSone UK Ltd.
EU unfair competition law Strong Lidsone operates in the EU targeting German speakers — the same market as ledsone.de. This constitutes an unfair commercial practice under Directive 2005/29/EC.
EU E-Commerce Directive breach Helps us No VAT number, no business address, no company registration — in breach of Article 5 of the EU E-Commerce Directive (2000/31/EC). This weakens any defence they may attempt.

3. Action Steps

Step 1 — Evidence Gathering (Do This Today)
  • Screenshot lidsone.com header/logo side by side with ledsone.co.uk
  • Archive the page: visit web.archive.org/save/https://lidsone.com
  • Save their logo file directly: lidsone.com/media/site_logos/lidsone.com_logo_raw.png
  • Record the date and time of discovery: 8 October 2026
Step 2 — WHOIS Lookup

Go to who.is/whois/lidsone.com to identify:

  • Domain registrar
  • Registration date (if after our brand launch = stronger case)
  • Hosting provider (for takedown request)
Step 3 — Send Cease & Desist Email

Send to: support@lidsone.com

Subject: Cease and Desist — Unauthorised Use of LEDSone Registered Logo
To Lidsone, We are LEDSone UK Ltd, the registered owner of the LEDSone brand and logo (ledsone.co.uk / ledsone.de). It has come to our attention that lidsone.com is displaying a logo that is identical or substantially similar to our registered brand identity. This constitutes copyright infringement and passing off under UK and EU law. We demand that you: 1. Immediately remove our logo from your website and all associated materials 2. Cease all use of our brand identity, logo, or trade dress 3. Confirm removal in writing within 7 days of this notice Failure to comply will result in us pursuing legal remedies including injunctive relief and damages. We reserve all rights. LEDSone UK Ltd ledsone.co.uk | ledsone.de admin@ledsone.de
Step 4 — Report to Hosting Platform & Registrar
  • Shopify (if applicable): shopify.com/legal/report-ip — Shopify acts quickly on IP complaints
  • Domain registrar: File IP abuse report (found via WHOIS in Step 2)
  • Hosting provider: File DMCA / IP takedown with their host
Step 5 — If No Response Within 7 Days
  • Escalate to a UK or EU intellectual property solicitor
  • Apply for injunctive relief to prevent continued infringement
  • File a formal complaint with the EUIPO (EU Intellectual Property Office) if trademark is registered
  • Pursue claim for damages and account of profits

4. Recommended Next Step

Check trademark registration status. If the LEDSone logo is formally registered as a UK or EU trademark, reference the registration number in all correspondence — it significantly strengthens the legal position and enables faster enforcement action through EUIPO and UK IPO.

If not yet registered, consider filing immediately at ipo.gov.uk (UK) or euipo.europa.eu (EU). Filing date creates a priority date even before registration is complete.
