  1. Your website lists 2,485 products — representing approximately 97.7% of our entire product catalogue — with identical titles, descriptions, specifications, and internal SKU numbers

  3. Trademark infringement — use of our registered brand name "LEDSONE" in your product listings
  4. Passing off — misrepresenting your business as connected to or endorsed by LEDSone UK Ltd
  5. Unfair commercial practice — systematic copying of a competitor's entire product database (EU Directive 2005/29/EC)
  6. Breach of EU E-Commerce Regulations — failure to display a valid business address, VAT number, or company registration (Article 5, EU Directive 2000/31/EC)
