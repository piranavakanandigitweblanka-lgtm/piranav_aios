## FAQs Generated

**1. What conduit pipe sizes are available?**
We stock conduit pipes and fittings in 16mm and 20mm diameters (also referred to as ¾" BSP). The 20mm pipes feature a 22mm outer diameter with female threaded ends and are available in lengths from 20cm to 80cm.

**2. What bulb fitting are the lamp holders compatible with?**
All conduit lamp holders and complete fixtures in this range use an E27 (ES) screw base. This is a standard UK/EU fitting, making it easy to pair with a wide variety of LED, filament, or vintage-style bulbs.

**3. What finishes are available for the lamp holders and fittings?**
Lamp holders are available in eight finishes: yellow brass, satin nickel, rose gold, French gold, black, copper, white, and chrome — so you can match or contrast your conduit pipe finish for a consistent industrial or vintage look.

**4. Can I build a complete light fitting using these components?**
Yes. The range is designed as a modular system — you can combine conduit pipes, elbows, tees, saddles, junction boxes, and lamp holders to build custom wall lights, ceiling pendants, or multi-arm fixtures from individual parts.

**5. Are the pendant lights compatible with a dimmer switch?**
Select pendant fixtures in the range, including the Industrial Fisherman Cage Pendants, are listed as dimmer compatible. Always check the individual product listing and ensure your chosen bulb is also dimmable for full compatibility.
